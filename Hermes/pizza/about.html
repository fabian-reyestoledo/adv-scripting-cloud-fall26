Our Story

Tom's Pizza started in 2010 as a single food truck with a wood-fired oven and a big dream. Founder Tom Marcello believed pizza should be simple, honest, and delicious — no shortcuts, no frozen dough, no secrets.

Today we run three locations and still make every crust by hand each morning. Our dough ferments for 48 hours for that perfect crispy-yet-chewy bite, and our signature sauce is a family recipe passed down for three generations.

Our Values

  • Quality: Only the freshest ingredients, sourced locally when possible.
  • Community: We sponsor local sports teams and host weekly pizza nights.
  • Craft: Every pie is hand-stretched and baked in our stone oven.

Come taste the difference — we promise you'll leave smiling and full.
